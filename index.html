Who We Are

We are an Eco friendliness charity based in Kettering, UK. We were established in 2012 by Greg Johnson who believes that the environment is the most important thing to protect. We firmly agree with him and try every day to Help The Earth that we live on.

What We Do

Our current aim is to help reduce the pollution and destruction of the natural environments that are around us all the time. This is important to us because we believe that the environment should be protected at all times and that pollution is a serious issue that should be stopped.

How You Can Help

There are many different ways that you can help the environment:

  • You can join in with events and help with the community efforts that we set up to help clean up the environment.
  • You can donate to allow us to continue working towards our goal for a cleaner planet.
  • You can spread the word on social media so that all your friends know.

Our Recent Events

Eco Friendly Friday

We set up a day where our volunteers spent the day trying to recycle as much of the non reusable waste that they create. Overall we recycled over 300 kg of plastic and saved 15kg of CO2 emissions from processing the waste .

Marine Life at Risk

78% of marine mammals are at risk from plastic pollution. 1 million die yearly.

Recycling Saves Energy

One aluminum can saves enough energy for 3 hours of TV.

Growing Food

Reduces distance food travels - 3 billion tonnes CO₂ annually globally.

Renewable Energy

By 2050, 80% of world energy could come from renewable sources.
